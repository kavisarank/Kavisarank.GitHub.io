01 / About Me

Driven by Purpose, Built by Design

I'm Kavisaran K, a 2nd-year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Dhirajlal Gandhi College of Technology, Salem — and I don't just study engineering, I live it through startups, competitions, and real-world innovation.

As the Founder & CEO of MechFlow Technology (MFT) — a Government of India MSME-registered company (UDYAM-TN-20-0172805) — I lead a team dedicated to building smart, sustainable mechanical systems.
